# Wushiland AI Social Content Assistant
## Project Scope

**Project Team:** I2AI Group 6  
**Client:** Wushiland  
**Project Period:** October–December 2026  
**Status:** Draft for Client Review and Approval  
**Project Management:** [GitHub Kanban Board](https://github.com/users/JocelynWang0414/projects/1/views/1)

### 1. Project Background and Need

Wushiland would like to strengthen its short-form social media presence and make it easier to develop timely, relevant content for its student audience, including during slower periods such as winter and summer breaks.

Identifying relevant trends, connecting them with local context, and translating them into usable content ideas currently requires manual research and planning. This project will explore how AI can support that process by combining social trend signals, local context, and Wushiland's brand considerations into practical content recommendations.

### 2. Project Objective

The objective is to develop a working prototype of an **AI-assisted social content planning tool** that helps Wushiland staff generate timely, brand-aligned short-form video ideas.

The tool will support staff decision-making rather than automate the full marketing process.

### 3. Proposed Approach

Users will be able to request content ideas for a selected time period. Depending on available data and technical feasibility, the system may consider:

- Campus events and academic calendars
- Weather, seasonal conditions, and local events
- Public or authorized social-media trend signals
- Relevant content examples and emerging formats
- Wushiland's brand guidelines and target audience

The system will combine these signals to suggest a small set of potential short-form video concepts. Recommendations may include a hook, content direction, script or shot suggestions, posting considerations, and a brief explanation of why the idea may be relevant.

Wushiland staff will remain responsible for reviewing, editing, approving, and publishing content.

### 4. Expected Deliverables by December

The team expects to provide:

- A functional prototype that generates social content recommendations
- A simple interface for requesting and reviewing recommendations
- A workflow for incorporating relevant contextual and trend information
- Brand-related guidance or guardrails where appropriate
- Reusable prompts, templates, or configuration materials
- A lightweight evaluation of recommendation usefulness and brand alignment
- Source code and basic technical documentation
- A final demonstration and project presentation

Specific features and implementation details may evolve based on data access, technical feasibility, and client feedback.

### 5. Scope Boundaries

The project focuses on **content ideation and planning** and does not currently include:

- Producing finished video files
- Automatically posting to Wushiland's social media accounts
- Dependence on unrestricted TikTok or Instagram data access
- Integration with Toast or other point-of-sale systems
- Formal measurement or attribution of sales, foot traffic, or marketing ROI
- Development of a production-scale marketing automation platform

Where appropriate, the prototype may use authorized APIs, public trend resources, third-party services, or a limited set of curated examples. Data sources will be selected based on accessibility, reliability, cost, and technical feasibility.

### 6. Key Constraints and Assumptions

This is an approximately two-month student project and will prioritize a demonstrable prototype over a production-ready system.

Some capabilities may depend on external APIs, account permissions, data availability, or third-party services outside the team's control. The project also assumes reasonable access to client feedback and relevant brand materials throughout development.

### 7. Client Support Requested

The team may request:

- Brand guidelines and examples of preferred content
- Relevant promotional, campus, or event calendars
- Examples of previous social media campaigns, if available
- Feedback on generated recommendations
- Guidance regarding confidentiality or NDA requirements
- Participation in milestone reviews and the final presentation

### 8. Project Success

By December, the project will be considered successful if the team can demonstrate an end-to-end prototype that uses relevant contextual information to generate content ideas that Wushiland staff consider reasonably useful and consistent with the brand.

The prototype is intended to demonstrate the feasibility and potential value of AI-assisted social content planning rather than prove a direct increase in sales or customer traffic.

### 9. Project Management and Review

The team will use the linked GitHub Kanban board to track major tasks, progress, blockers, and client-review items throughout the project.

This scope reflects the team's understanding of the initial client requirements and is being provided to Wushiland for review. It may be refined based on client feedback and findings during development.
